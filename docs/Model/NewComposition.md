**database_schema** | **object** | The tables this composition keeps what visitors send it in (votes, ratings, answers). They are made when the composition is created, in a schema of their own that no other composition can reach: in Widgetic&#39;s database, or in your own Neon database when you have set one with &#x60;setPartnerDataDatabase&#x60;. They are deleted with the composition.  The shape is &#x60;{ \&quot;tables\&quot;: [ ... ] }&#x60;. A table has a &#x60;name&#x60;, a list of &#x60;columns&#x60; and, optionally, a list of &#x60;indexes&#x60;, and optionally &#x60;countable&#x60;. A column has a &#x60;name&#x60; and a &#x60;type&#x60;, and optionally &#x60;primaryKey&#x60;, &#x60;notNull&#x60;, &#x60;unique&#x60;, &#x60;default&#x60; and &#x60;readable&#x60;. An index has a &#x60;name&#x60; and the &#x60;columns&#x60; it covers (1 to 8), and optionally &#x60;unique&#x60;. Any other key is refused, except &#x60;source&#x60; (see below).  &#x60;countable&#x60; and &#x60;readable&#x60; are the read policy of the composition&#39;s own page: nothing the visitors sent can be read back by the page until you say so. &#x60;countable: true&#x60; on a table lets the widget show how many rows it has; &#x60;readable: true&#x60; on a column lets it show or total that column&#39;s values (a tally of votes, an average rating). Mark only what is safe to show to anyone who opens the page. A column whose name holds &#x60;email&#x60;, &#x60;phone&#x60;, &#x60;address&#x60;, &#x60;token&#x60;, &#x60;password&#x60;, &#x60;secret&#x60;, &#x60;ip&#x60; or the like, and a &#x60;json&#x60;, &#x60;jsonb&#x60; or &#x60;uuid&#x60; column, cannot be &#x60;readable&#x60;.  - Names (tables, columns, indexes) use lower-case letters, digits and underscores, start with a letter and   are at most 63 characters long. A name that starts with &#x60;_&#x60; or &#x60;pg_&#x60; is reserved. - &#x60;type&#x60; is one of &#x60;text&#x60;, &#x60;smallint&#x60;, &#x60;integer&#x60; (or &#x60;int&#x60;), &#x60;bigint&#x60;, &#x60;numeric&#x60;, &#x60;real&#x60;,   &#x60;double precision&#x60;, &#x60;boolean&#x60; (or &#x60;bool&#x60;), &#x60;date&#x60;, &#x60;timestamp&#x60;, &#x60;timestamptz&#x60; (or   &#x60;timestamp with time zone&#x60;), &#x60;uuid&#x60;, &#x60;json&#x60;, &#x60;jsonb&#x60;, &#x60;serial&#x60;, &#x60;bigserial&#x60;. - &#x60;default&#x60; is a number, &#x60;true&#x60; or &#x60;false&#x60;, a text of at most 500 characters (no backslash), a JSON value   for a &#x60;json&#x60; or &#x60;jsonb&#x60; column, or one of &#x60;now()&#x60;, &#x60;current_timestamp&#x60; and &#x60;current_date&#x60; (date and   timestamp columns) and &#x60;gen_random_uuid()&#x60; (a &#x60;uuid&#x60; column). A &#x60;serial&#x60; column numbers itself and has   no default. - A composition declares at most 20 tables, with at most 64 columns and 10 indexes each.  A declaration that cannot be used is refused with &#x60;400&#x60; and &#x60;INVALID_DATABASE_SCHEMA&#x60;; &#x60;error.details.problems&#x60; lists what to change. Tables that already exist are never changed or dropped: sending more tables later, when you update the composition, adds the ones that are missing. When the composition is saved but its tables cannot be made just now, the answer says so in &#x60;meta.warnings&#x60;: send the same &#x60;databaseSchema&#x60; again to retry.  Leave it out and the composition gets the tables its widget declares in its own source (&#x60;src/lib/meta/database.json&#x60;), if any. Send &#x60;{ \&quot;tables\&quot;: [] }&#x60; to give the composition no tables and keep it from getting its widget&#39;s.  A composition whose tables were made from its widget&#39;s own file reads back with &#x60;\&quot;source\&quot;: \&quot;widget\&quot;&#x60; next to &#x60;tables&#x60;. When the widget is published again, a table, a field or an index its file gained is added to them; nothing that exists is changed or dropped, and what a page may read does not change. Send &#x60;source&#x60; back as it was and it stays. Leave it out and the tables are yours: no widget&#39;s file adds anything to them. It is the only value &#x60;source&#x60; can have. | [optional]
